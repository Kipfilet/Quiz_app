QUIZ RULES

Before you start

Take a moment to understand how the quiz works. Your score depends on both accuracy and question difficulty.

01

You have three lives

You begin every quiz with three hearts. Each incorrect answer removes one heart.

02

Earn points for correct answers

Every correct answer increases your score. The number of points depends on the difficulty of the question.

Easy · 10 pts Normal · 15 pts Hard · 20 pts

03

Questions have different difficulty levels

Questions are divided into three difficulty levels: Easy, Normal, and Hard. Harder questions reward more points.

Easy Normal Hard

04

Losing all your lives ends the quiz

If you answer incorrectly three times, all your hearts will be gone and the quiz will end.

No hearts left means game over.
